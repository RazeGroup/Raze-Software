Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At RAZE Software, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and corporate information when you access our platforms, software systems, and CapitalField solution.

Information We Collect

Personal & Corporate Data

  • Account credentials (name, business email, phone number, company name)
  • System usage logs and session authentication data
  • Billing and subscription details
  • Communication records with RAZE support

Multi-Tenant & Field Data Security

Data stored within CapitalField is strictly isolated per company tenant. All session cookies are encrypted with httpOnly flags and parameterized database isolation.

How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver, maintain, and optimize software systems and CapitalField modules
  • To handle tenant provisioning, role permissions, and administrative access
  • To provide technical support and software updates
  • To enforce cloud security and safeguard multi-tenant isolation

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our team at contact@raze-software.com.
