How CapitalField Works

Four simple steps to bring your entire service operation under control with RAZE system software.

01

Set Up Your Company

Create your company profile on CapitalField, add your team members, assign roles (Admin, Manager, Technician), and install an industry template that matches your business sector — water filters, HVAC, elevators, and more. Everything is configured automatically.

02

Add Your Customers

Build your customer database with contact details, addresses, sectors, and notes. Attach contracts, set renewal dates, and add any custom fields your business requires. Every customer gets a full profile with complete visit history.

03

Schedule & Manage Visits

Assign visits to technicians with a date, time, and base price. Technicians update visit status in real-time (Scheduled → In Progress → Completed). After every visit, a sector-specific report form is filled out and stored automatically.

04

Track, Report & Grow

Use the Reports module to track monthly visits and revenue, identify underperforming technicians, and spot overdue contracts. The dashboard surfaces the most important numbers every time you log in — so you always know exactly where your business stands.
