Smart Dashboard

Customer CRM

Visit Management

Schedule, assign, and track every field visit. Custom statuses, inline status updates, visit reports per sector, and full CRUD.

Technician Management

View technician cards with specialty, team, availability, completion rate, star rating, and total visits at a glance.

Reports & Analytics

User & Role Management

Calendar View

Industry Templates
