Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RAZE and CapitalField.

CapitalField is a cloud-based field service management platform developed by RAZE for service companies — water filter companies, HVAC technicians, elevator maintenance teams, pest control businesses, and more. It helps you manage customers, schedule visits, track technicians, handle contracts, and generate reports — all from one dashboard.

CapitalField is built for any company that sends technicians or service teams to customer locations. This includes water filter installation/maintenance, HVAC, pest control, elevator maintenance, security systems, solar energy, home appliances, and cleaning companies. If you have a field team, CapitalField is built for you.

Yes. CapitalField is built as a multi-tenant platform. Each company's data is fully isolated and secure. Admins can manage multiple company profiles from a single dashboard, making it ideal for business groups or management companies overseeing multiple branches.

Industry templates are pre-configured setups for specific service sectors. When you install a template (e.g. Water Filters), CapitalField automatically creates the right custom fields, visit statuses, and report forms for that industry. This means your team can start working from day one without manual configuration.

Yes — fully. The Settings panel lets you add unlimited custom fields (text, number, date, select, checkbox, file) to any entity. You can also create custom visit statuses with any name and color. Everything is tailored to how your business actually works.

CapitalField has built-in system roles: Super Admin, Admin, Manager, Customer Service, and Technician. Each role has a defined set of permissions. You can also create custom roles and assign granular permissions (e.g. visits.edit or customers.*). This ensures every team member sees only what they need.
