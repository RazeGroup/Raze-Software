FAQ

Common questions about our engineering practices and software ecosystem.

What is RAZE Software?

We are a software engineering firm focused on building high-performance, non-redundant enterprise applications and operational systems.

What does "Nothing redundant" mean?

Our core engineering philosophy. We do not ship bloatware, we do not over-engineer features that clients won't use, and we optimize for speed, security, and scalability from day one.

What is CapitalField?

CapitalField is our flagship SaaS offering — a multi-tenant Field Service Management system tailored for companies running field operations (HVAC, elevators, pest control, etc.).

Do you build custom software for clients?

Yes. While we maintain our own product ecosystem, our engineering team partners with enterprises to build custom architecture tailored to specific business requirements.
